# Implementation roadmap
1. Stage 2 (by 4 Dec): blueprint pack, working prototype already live.
2. Stage 3 (by 8 Dec): 1 km forest-masked grid, slope and road distance layers, sourced water factor, 5-minute walkthrough video.
3. Stage 4 (by 11 Dec): state-wide deployment guide (cloud, on-prem, edge), load test, business case, reproducibility pack.
4. Pilot (Feb-Jun 2027 fire season): 2-3 ranges in Nainital or Almora divisions with the state forest department, SMS to beat guards.
5. Scale: other fire-prone states (Odisha, Madhya Pradesh, Mizoram, Andhra Pradesh) with the same pipeline.
