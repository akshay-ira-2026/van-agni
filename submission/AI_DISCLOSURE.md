# AI and data tools used (for Rule G5 disclosure)
- Ira (AI agent): wrote the data pipeline, model code, dashboard, tests and documents, under the team's direction. Team reviewed and owns the output.
- LightGBM (open-source ML library): the risk model itself. Not generative.
