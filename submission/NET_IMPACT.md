# Net-impact statement (Rule G2)
## Incurred
- Under 0.002 kgCO2e for all development training; about 2.6e-03 gCO2e a year for daily scoring.

## Avoided (method, not a claim)
- Each hectare of forest fire avoided saves the carbon in burned litter and biomass. Method: avoided ha × emission factor per ha (IPCC (Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change) 2006 Vol 4 Ch 2 tables for tropical dry forest).
- Pathway: earlier patrols at the right cells shorten time to first response, so fewer small fires grow large.
- Pilot measure to collect: hectares burned and response time in pilot ranges vs neighbouring ranges, one season.

## Ratio
- Even one hectare avoided in a season outweighs the system's yearly compute emissions by more than a million times. The ratio will be computed from pilot data, not assumed.

## Rebound
- Risk of alert fatigue (staff ignore alerts) and risk of over-patrolling with vehicles. Mitigation: only top 10 cells a day, reasons shown, foot patrols preferred.
