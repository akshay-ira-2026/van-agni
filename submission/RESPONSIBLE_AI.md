# Responsible AI declaration (Rule G4)
- Provenance and rights: NASA FIRMS and NASA POWER (NASA open data, free use with credit), Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0), OpenStreetMap (ODbL). Credits shown on the map and README.
- Personal data: none collected or processed.
- Fairness: risk is spatial. Remote or cloud-covered cells have fewer recorded fires and can be under-ranked. Checked per region; weather features reduce this.
- Model card: MODEL_CARD.md.
- Human oversight: advisory only. Every alert carries "verify with beat staff". No automated dispatch, blame or enforcement.
- Misuse guard: output is about places, never people; not to be used to accuse villagers of setting fires.
