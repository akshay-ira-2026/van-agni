# Business case (draft for Stage 4)
- Users: state forest departments (range and divisional forest officers), Van Panchayats, JFM (Joint Forest Management) committees.
- Cost to run: zero on free hosting; under one hour a year of maintenance per state after setup.
- Model: open-source core (free for government), paid setup and training support per state, and an SMS add-on billed at cost.
- Funding routes: CAMPA (Compensatory Afforestation Fund Management and Planning Authority) funds, state disaster management, CSR (Corporate Social Responsibility) climate grants.
